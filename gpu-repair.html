// Common GPU Problems

Sound Familiar?

These are the graphics card faults we repair every week at board level.

⛔
PC won't turn on with the card installed

Remove the GPU and the PC starts. Most often a shorted MOSFET, capacitor or power stage on the card — very repairable.

🖥️
No display / no signal

Fans spin but nothing on screen, or the card isn't detected. We trace the fault to the failed rail or component.

🌈
Artifacts, crashes, driver errors

Coloured lines, squares, black screens in games or "Code 43". Can point to memory or power faults.

🔥
Burnt power connector

Melted 12VHPWR / 8-pin connector or burnt pins. We replace the connector and check the board behind it.

🌡️
Overheating / loud fans

High hotspot temps or thermal throttling. Fresh PTM7950 on the core and new thermal pads.

// Before You Install

GPU Install Tips

Most of the dead cards we see could have been avoided. Check these every time you install or move a graphics card.

🔌
Push the power connector all the way in

Especially 12VHPWR / 12V-2x6 on RTX 40 & 50 cards. It must click in with no gap. A half-seated plug is the #1 cause of melted connectors.

↪️
Don't bend the cable at the plug

Keep the power cable straight for about 35 mm before any bend. A sharp bend right at the connector pulls the pins loose.

Use separate PCIe cables

For cards with two or more 8-pin plugs, run a separate cable from the PSU to each one instead of one daisy-chained cable.

Power off and unplug first

Switch off the PSU and pull the power cord before installing or removing the card. Never hot-plug a GPU.

Support heavy cards

Lock the PCIe slot latch, screw in the bracket and use a support stand. GPU sag can crack solder joints and the PCIe area over time.

🖥️
Plug the monitor into the GPU

Use the graphics card's outputs, not the motherboard's. Many "no display" cards are actually fine.

// FAQ

GPU Repair Questions

How much does GPU repair cost at Computer Geeks Repair?

Labour starts at $350 and goes up to $650 depending on the fault (parts and tax extra). If the card can't be repaired you only pay a $45 diagnostic fee.

My PC won't turn on when the graphics card is installed. Can it be fixed?

Usually, yes. This is most often a shorted component on the card's power circuit, which makes the power supply shut off to protect itself. It's one of the most common faults we repair.

Is repairing worth it vs. buying a new card?

For cards like the RTX 3060 up to the RTX 4090, a board-level repair usually costs far less than a replacement. Labour is $350–$650 depending on the fault, so you know the range up front.

How long does a GPU repair take?

It depends on the fault and whether parts need to be ordered. Ask us at drop-off and we'll give you an estimate.

Which cards do you repair?

NVIDIA GeForce RTX cards from all brands (ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte, Zotac, EVGA and more). Have a different card? Send us the model and we'll let you know.

Do I need to bring my whole PC?

No, just the graphics card is fine.
